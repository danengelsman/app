Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Cancel Any Time

You may cancel your Vertano subscription at any time with no penalties or cancellation fees. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which it will not renew.

How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription in two ways:

  1. Via account settings: Log in to Vertano, navigate to your Account Settings, and select "Cancel Subscription."
  2. Via email: Send a cancellation request to support@agenticsolutions.io with your account email. We will process the cancellation within 1 business day and send you a confirmation.

What Happens After Cancellation

  • You retain full access to Vertano until the end of your paid billing period
  • No further charges will be made after cancellation
  • Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted
  • You may re-subscribe at any time; data from previous sessions may not be recoverable after deletion

Timing

To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, cancel before your renewal date. The exact renewal date is shown in your account settings. Cancellations submitted after the renewal date apply to the following billing period.

Pausing Your Subscription

If you need a break but don't want to lose your data and settings, contact us at support@agenticsolutions.io. We offer subscription pauses of up to 3 months for qualifying accounts.

Contact

Questions about cancellation? Email support@agenticsolutions.io — we're happy to help.
